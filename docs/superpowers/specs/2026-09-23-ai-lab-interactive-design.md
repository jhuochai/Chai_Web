# AI lab interactive showcase

Approved in this task: the navy spacecraft workroom mockup, two physical monitors, warm task lighting, no decorative double frames or corner rivets. Clicking a monitor or its primary action expands a near-fullscreen interactive demo; close/Escape returns to the triggering device. On mobile the demo fills the viewport. This approval authorizes implementation without another design gate.

The livestream demo has overview, schedule, session details, editable checkable tasks, and appearance selection. The lyrics demo has a timed original-text preview, play/pause, seeking, opacity, translation, drag and lock controls. It does not claim to play audio or control other desktop apps. Demo data is fictional and stays in memory; reset restores defaults.

Preserve Chinese/English, Stapu and capability records, route controls and existing transition fixes. Provide making-of content and Windows portable-download instructions. Release archives are local public assets, not automatically published. No account credentials or personal application data enter the demo or downloads.

Accessibility: visible focus, focus containment and restoration, Escape, inert background, scroll locking, touch targets, reduced-motion alternative. Functional inputs and copy are HTML, never baked into the room image.
